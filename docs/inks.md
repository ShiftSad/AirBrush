# Inks & cauldrons

Quills draw with **ink** and cloths erase when soaked with **solvent**. Both are
brewed in an ordinary water cauldron.

## Brewing in a cauldron

1. **Fill a cauldron with water.**
2. **Throw the ingredients into the water** (drop them so the items land inside the
   cauldron). After a short moment the water takes on a colour and the brew is ready —
   you'll see witch particles when it locks in.
3. **Dip your tool**: right-click the cauldron with the matching tool to fill it.
   Each dip consumes **one** water level, so a full cauldron is good for three dips.

!!! info "Grace window"
    The cauldron waits about 1.5 seconds after the last item lands before deciding the
    recipe, so multi-ingredient inks aren't mistaken for a simpler subset. Throw all
    the ingredients in together.

## Inks

Throw the ingredients into a water cauldron to brew the ink. **Glows** marks inks
whose strokes shine in the dark; **Permanent** marks inks whose strokes never fade.

| Ink | Ingredients | Glows | Permanent |
| --- | --- | :---: | :---: |
| Common Ink | 1 ink sac | — | — |
| Amethyst Ink | 1 amethyst shard | — | — |
| Luminous Ink | 1 glow ink sac + 1 amethyst shard | ✅ | — |
| Indelible Ink | 1 echo shard + 1 diamond + 1 amethyst block | — | ✅ |
| Luminous Indelible Ink | 1 echo shard + 1 diamond + 1 amethyst block + 1 glow ink sac | ✅ | ✅ |

## Solvent

| Solvent | Ingredients |
| --- | --- |
| Solvent | 1 slime ball + 1 fermented spider eye |

Dip a **cloth** into a solvent cauldron to soak it. A wet cloth can erase; once it
dries out, soak it again.

## Tinting the colour

A brewed ink starts at its natural colour, but you can **shade it with dyes**: throw
any of the 16 vanilla dye items into the same cauldron and each one pulls the colour
halfway toward its own hue. Throw several to mix — the water surface previews the
result.

??? note "The 16 dyes"
    White, Light Gray, Gray, Black, Brown, Red, Orange, Yellow, Lime, Green, Cyan,
    Light Blue, Blue, Purple, Magenta, Pink.

## Which tool takes which liquid

Each tool only accepts the liquid it's meant for — dip the wrong one and nothing
happens.

| Tool | Accepts | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amethyst Dye (marker) | **Amethyst Ink** only | The marker is the amethyst-only pen. |
| Quill | **any ink except Amethyst** | Common, Luminous, Indelible, Luminous Indelible. |
| Cloth | **Solvent** only | Soaks the cloth so it can erase. |

!!! warning "Switching inks in a quill"
    Dipping a quill into a *different* ink than it currently holds asks for
    confirmation — right-click again within a few seconds to confirm. Replacing the
    ink **empties whatever was left** in the quill before refilling.
